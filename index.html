Grown-up corner

Batteries only, 9 volts or less, never wall power. Coin batteries and strong magnets stay with grown-ups: both are very dangerous if swallowed. The soldering iron is a grown-up tool.

How the game shows electricity

The yellow dots are electrons. They move out of the battery's minus end and back into its plus end, which is how electrons really travel. Circuit diagrams usually draw "conventional current" the other way, from plus to minus. Both describe the same loop.

The circuits are worked out by a small simulator: a 3 V battery, bulbs, LEDs that only conduct one way and burn out without a resistor, capacitors that charge and discharge, and a transistor switched by a tiny base current.
